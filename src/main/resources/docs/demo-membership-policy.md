# Cedar Grove Learning Center: Membership Policy

This is fictional sample content for testing the DocsChat application.

## Membership plans

The learning center offers a free Community plan and a Plus plan that costs $12 per month. Both plans allow members to borrow up to three books at a time.

Plus members may borrow up to six books at a time and may reserve a study room up to seven days in advance. Community members may reserve a study room up to two days in advance.

## Joining and cancellation

Anyone may join at the front desk with a name and an email address. A guardian must register members under the age of 16.

Plus membership can be cancelled at any time at the front desk or by emailing membership@cedargrove.example. Cancellation takes effect at the end of the current billing month. The center does not offer partial-month refunds.

## Borrowing rules

Books may be borrowed for 21 days and renewed once if no other member has placed a hold. Overdue books have a five-day grace period. After the grace period, the late fee is $0.25 per book per day, up to a maximum of $8 per book.
