# Cedar Grove Learning Center: Classes and Study Rooms

This is fictional sample content for testing the DocsChat application.

## Weekly classes

The center holds a beginner gardening class every Tuesday from 5:30 p.m. to 6:30 p.m. in Garden Room. The class is free, but registration is required because the room has 12 seats.

A creative writing workshop meets every Thursday from 4:00 p.m. to 5:30 p.m. in Studio B. The workshop is open to adults and teens aged 15 or older. Participants should bring a notebook; other materials are provided.

Register for either class at the front desk or by calling 555-0142. Registration opens two weeks before each class.

## Study rooms

There are four study rooms, each suitable for up to four people. Room bookings are limited to two hours per day per member. A member who is more than 15 minutes late may lose the reservation.

Study rooms are available during regular center hours. They cannot be booked on public holidays.
