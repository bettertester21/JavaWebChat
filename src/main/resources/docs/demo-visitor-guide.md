# Cedar Grove Learning Center: Visitor Guide

This is fictional sample content for testing the DocsChat application.

## Hours and location

Cedar Grove Learning Center is open Monday through Friday from 9:00 a.m. to 6:00 p.m. It is closed on Saturdays and Sundays. The center is located at 25 Garden Lane, Brookfield.

## Visitor services

Visitors can use the reading room without a reservation. The public Wi-Fi network is named `CedarGuest`. Ask the front desk for the daily Wi-Fi passphrase.

The center has a small cafe that serves drinks and snacks from 10:00 a.m. to 4:30 p.m. Outside food is allowed in the garden seating area, but not in the reading room.

## Contact

For general questions, call the front desk at 555-0142 during opening hours. The center does not provide services on public holidays.
